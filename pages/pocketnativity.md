**Welcome to Pocket Nativity, a cozy 2.5D Nativity diorama creator and animated Christmas postcard maker for the whole family.**

Design your own interactive Bethlehem scene in five simple steps—from sculpting multi-layered hills and rivers to placing the Holy Family, the Three Wise Men, shepherds, and festive decorations. Once your Nativity is ready, share it as a living, animated postcard link with family and friends via WhatsApp, email, or social media—no app installation required to watch!

We believe digital holiday traditions should feel warm, creative, and stress-free. That is why **Pocket Nativity** is built with the same **"Calm Tech"** philosophy as all of our creations: no ads, no data tracking, and pure creative joy.

## Our Philosophy: Festive & Calm Tech

**🛡️ Safe & Private by Design**
100% Ad-Free. No In-App Purchases. No Data Tracking. Create and share your Christmas greetings with total peace of mind.

**✨ Interactive 2.5D Diorama Builder**
Shape symmetrical multi-plane hills, choose between sand, snow, and grassy biomes, light up the night sky, and trigger charming character animations with a single tap.

**💌 Living Postcards via Link**
Unlike static images, every card you share opens an animated web scene complete with falling snowglobe flakes, warm lanterns, and festive music.
